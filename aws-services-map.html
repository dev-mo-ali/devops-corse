03 / Learn

Suggested learning roadmap

For software engineers and DevOps practitioners: master the infrastructure foundations, then deepen platform operations and specialize.

PHASE 01

Core infrastructure

VPC, EC2, ALB, Auto Scaling, S3, RDS, IAM, Route 53 and CloudWatch.

PHASE 02

Production operations

ECS/EKS, SSM, CloudTrail, Config, WAF, secrets, SQS, IaC and CI/CD.

PHASE 03

Advanced architecture

Multi-account governance, hybrid networks, analytics, Bedrock, disaster recovery and cost optimization.
